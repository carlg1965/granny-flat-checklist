Why community partnerships

Since 15 January 2026, a granny flat of up to 70m² can be built without a building consent. Council no longer checks the work along the way — but it must still meet the Building Code.

Many of the families building these homes are multigenerational households from Chinese, Punjabi, Hindi-speaking, Pasifika and other communities. Building advice often travels by word of mouth, through the people families already trust. Our Partnership Teams put clear, accurate checklists into those conversations.

The teams

Community Language Partners

One trusted bilingual person for each language — ideally an LBP, inspector, designer or experienced builder.

  • Own and review the building-terms glossary for your language
  • Approve corrections that users suggest
  • Share the checklist through your community channels

Builders & LBPs

Give your clients a clear record of each construction milestone, with an optional LBP sign-off on every stage.

  • Show clients your work was checked stage by stage
  • A referral link for clients who buy the checklist

Granny flat suppliers & brokers

Kitset suppliers, builders of granny flats and mortgage brokers can offer the checklist as part of their service.

  • Signed PDF records your clients can share with lenders and insurers
  • Bundled or referral options

Community groups

Marae, churches, temples, gurdwaras, cultural associations and residents' groups.

  • Free talks and Q&A sessions on the new granny flat rules
  • Printed guides to the free Siting checklist

What partners receive

  • Recognition on this site for your role and your community.
  • A personal referral code — a share of every sale made with your code, paid quarterly.
  • Discounted access to every checklist stage for your own projects or clients.
  • First access to LBP Pro, our coming professional tier with full Building Code references and branded records.

Partners who share a referral link tell their audience they receive a commission. Honest promotion protects everyone.
