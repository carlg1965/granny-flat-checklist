What it does

Since January 2026, New Zealanders can build a granny flat up to 70m² without a building consent. That means no council inspector checks the work — but it still has to meet the Building Code. The Granny Flat Checklist puts you in control: plain-language, stage-by-stage self-checks you complete on site with your LBP, from siting to final, each with a signed PDF record. Written by a former Auckland building inspector.

The risk with exempt work

Exempt building work still has to meet the Building Code — nobody signs off before you build, which means nobody catches a mistake until it's already in the ground or under the cladding. This checklist walks you through what a building inspector actually checks, stage by stage, before you close each one up.
